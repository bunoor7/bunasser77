Build this animation as a single self-contained HTML file (inline CSS and vanilla JS, canvas 2D only, no libraries).

The animation:
Notifications. Three notification cards stack up on a light background. Each card (620 × 120, radius 32, white, soft shadow) drops in from 44 px above with ease out and a slight scale up, and pushes the earlier cards down by 140 px. In order: "New prompt saved" with "blueprint-draw.md" and 1H (black icon tile with a sparkle), then "Zee left a comment" with "Slow the hero reveal" and 2M (black tile with a dot), then "Render complete" with "50 clips exported" and NOW (orange tile with a tick that draws itself, plus an orange unread dot that lands). They arrive at t 0.03, 0.3 and 0.57. [Swap in your three messages]

Setup:
- One canvas with a 912 × 600 drawing space, centred on the page, scaled to fit the window and sharp on retina screens.
- Write the whole animation as one function draw(ctx, t), where t runs from 0 to 1 and every frame depends only on t.
- Play t from 0 to 1 over 2 seconds, hold the last frame for 1 second, then loop.
- Easing words I use above: "ease out" is cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), "glide" is cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1), and "lands" means a small overshoot, cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1).
