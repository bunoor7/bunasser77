Build this animation as a single self-contained HTML file (inline CSS and vanilla JS, canvas 2D only, no libraries).

The animation:
Phone Trio. Three realistic phones stand side by side in 3D on a light background: a centre phone facing the viewer and slightly forward, and two side phones set back and turned about 30° toward the centre. Each phone has a metal edge, a thin black bezel, a pill-shaped camera cutout and a status bar. Each screen shows a different app screen: a home feed in the centre, a profile with stats and a photo grid on the left, and a dark analytics screen with a line chart and bars on the right. The phones rise 140 px into place one after another with ease out as they fade in, then float gently while the whole group sways a few degrees. Soft blurred shadows sit under each phone. Draw the screen to an off-screen canvas first, then map it onto the phone's face in perspective by splitting it into a grid of small triangles, each drawn with an affine transform and a clip.

Setup:
- One canvas with a 912 × 600 drawing space, centred on the page, scaled to fit the window and sharp on retina screens.
- Write the whole animation as one function draw(ctx, t), where t runs from 0 to 1 and every frame depends only on t.
- Play t from 0 to 1 over 2 seconds, hold the last frame for 1 second, then loop.
- Easing words I use above: "ease out" is cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), "glide" is cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1), and "lands" means a small overshoot, cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1).

Brand:

Check before you finish:
- Nothing clips at the canvas edges and the text is sharp.
- The last frame works as a still image.
